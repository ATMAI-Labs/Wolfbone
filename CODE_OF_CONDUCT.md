# Community conduct

Wolfbone welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, abilities, levels of mathematical training, and ways of communicating.

## Working together

- Treat people with respect. Critique ideas, claims, and evidence with care.
- Welcome plain language, diagrams, assistive tools, and requests for another explanation. Mathematical fluency is not a condition of belonging.
- Make room for disagreement and correction. Explain assumptions and distinguish evidence from interpretation.
- Credit others' contributions and respect their privacy.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual attention or content directed at others without consent, and disclosure of personal information without permission.
- Do not use project spaces for impersonation, spam, or intimidation.

These expectations apply in Wolfbone's repository, discussions, and other official project spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting concerns

A monitored private reporting contact must be published here before public launch. While the repository is private, collaborators should use their established private contact with Atmai's project maintainers. Do not post sensitive personal details in issues or discussions.

The missing public reporting contact is tracked in the [public launch checklist](docs/PUBLIC_LAUNCH.md). Do not treat a public issue as a confidential reporting channel.

## Maintainer response

Atmai's maintainers will consider reports fairly and share sensitive details only as needed to address the concern. Responses may include clarification, removal of content, a warning, or temporary or permanent limits on participation, depending on the conduct and its impact.

A person involved in a report should not be its sole decision-maker. Concerns about a response may be raised with another Atmai maintainer when available. The project does not currently promise a specific response time or a staffed reporting service.
